Bael Plant

Plant Information

Botanical Name: Aegle marmelos (L.) Corrêa

Common Name: Bael / Bengal quince

Family: Rutaceae

Description

A small to medium-sized tree with trifoliate leaves and hard, round fruits.

Medicinal Uses

  1. Traditionally used for digestive problems and to support gut health.
  2. Helps in managing diarrhoea and dysentery.
  3. Traditionally used for digestive weakness.
